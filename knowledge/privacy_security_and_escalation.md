# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Privacy, Security, and Escalation

## Privacy Principles
- Verify the customer's identity.
- Share only information required for the support purpose.
- Use the minimum necessary personal data.
- Do not disclose information belonging to another customer.
- Avoid displaying full payment credentials, passwords, authentication codes, or security answers.

## Escalation Categories
Escalate when the case involves:
- Suspected fraud or account takeover
- Privacy or identity concerns
- Unauthorized account changes
- Financial adjustment or refund approval
- Regulatory or legal complaint
- Repeated system failure
- Missing or contradictory source data
- Service-impacting incidents requiring specialist teams

## Escalation Record
Include:
- Customer and account reference
- Issue summary
- Business impact
- Evidence reviewed
- Actions already taken
- Exact unresolved question
- Required team or approval
- Customer communication already provided
