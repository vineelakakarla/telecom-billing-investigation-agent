# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Products, Subscriptions, and Orders

## Scope
Covers mobile, broadband, fixed-line, TV, digital, and converged products.

## Product and Service Concepts
- Product offering: the commercial definition available for sale.
- Product: the offering subscribed to by a customer.
- Service: the technical service supporting the product.
- Resource: the infrastructure or identifier used by the service.
- Order: a request to create, modify, suspend, resume, or terminate a product.
- Subscription: the customer's active commercial relationship with a product.

## Common Lifecycle Events
- New activation
- Upgrade or downgrade
- Add-on activation
- Suspension and resumption
- Relocation
- Transfer of ownership
- Cancellation
- Reconnection
- Renewal
- Contract or commitment change

## Key Checks
- Product and service identifiers
- Order status
- Effective date
- Activation or cancellation date
- Recurring versus one-time charge
- Proration
- Equipment or installation fee
- Dependency between products and services

## Support Response
Explain the relationship between the order or subscription change and the resulting charge. Distinguish requested, completed, failed, and pending changes.
