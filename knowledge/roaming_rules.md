# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Roaming

## Scope
Covers domestic and international roaming, voice, SMS, data, roaming packs, and partner-network usage.

## Key Checks
- Customer location or visited network
- Roaming zone or country
- Event date and time
- Service type and usage quantity
- Active roaming pack
- Pack allowance and expiry
- Applicable tariff
- Rating and billing timestamps
- Whether the usage was inside or outside the subscribed bundle

## Common Scenarios
- Roaming was enabled and usage was charged at the applicable tariff.
- A roaming pack had expired before usage occurred.
- Usage exceeded the pack allowance.
- Usage occurred in a country outside the pack's coverage.
- Partner records arrived after the normal billing cycle.
- Incoming roaming, voicemail, background data, or application traffic generated charges.

## Support Response
Explain the visited location, event, applicable pack or tariff, usage quantity, and billed amount. If the event is not available, request the usage or partner-record details.
