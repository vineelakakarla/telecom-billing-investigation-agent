# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Customer and Account Management

## Scope
Covers customer identity, accounts, contacts, ownership, and account-level support actions.

## Key Concepts
- Individual customer
- Business customer
- Customer organization
- Billing account
- Service account
- Account owner
- Authorized contact
- Contact medium
- Account status

## Key Checks
- Customer and account identifiers
- Account ownership
- Authorized contact status
- Account status
- Billing and service addresses
- Preferred contact channel
- Consent and communication preferences

## Support Controls
- Verify identity before disclosing account information.
- Confirm authorization before changing ownership, payment details, or sensitive contact information.
- Keep customer and account data separate from internal notes.
- Do not expose authentication secrets or full payment credentials.
- Escalate ownership, fraud, identity, and privacy concerns.
