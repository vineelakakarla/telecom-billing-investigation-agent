Telecom Billing Knowledge

Roaming Charges
Roaming charges are generated when a customer uses telecom services outside
their home network or country. These charges may include voice calls,
SMS messages, and mobile data usage.

Late-Rated Events
A late-rated event is a usage event that occurred in an earlier billing
period but was processed or rated later. The charge may therefore appear
on the customer's current bill even though the usage happened previously.

Higher Bill Investigation
When a customer's bill is higher than the previous bill, the CSR should
compare the bills and check usage charges, roaming charges, new subscriptions,
discounts, taxes, one-time charges, and duplicate charges.

Bill Comparison
A bill comparison should identify changes in the bill total, individual
bill items, usage quantity, applicable rates, discounts, taxes, and
additional charges.

Discount Expiration
A bill may increase when a promotional discount or customer-specific
discount expires.

New Subscription
A newly activated subscription may introduce a recurring charge or
activation-related charge on the customer's bill.

Human Review
Human review is required when the available billing information is
insufficient, the customer disputes an unrecognized charge, or the
investigation cannot establish a reliable root cause.
