# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Discounts, Credits, and Promotions

## Scope
Covers promotional offers, loyalty discounts, bundles, credits, and adjustments.

## Key Checks
- Promotion or discount identifier
- Eligibility criteria
- Start and end dates
- Number of promotional billing cycles
- Product or plan association
- Discount amount and application status
- Whether a credit was applied, reversed, or moved to another bill

## Common Scenarios
- Promotion expired.
- Customer changed plan or product.
- Eligibility conditions were no longer met.
- Bundle discount was removed.
- Discount was delayed or applied to a later bill.
- Credit was issued as a separate adjustment.
- Discount was reversed because of a correction.

## Support Response
Explain whether the increase came from a new charge or a reduced credit. Do not promise reinstatement without checking eligibility and authorization rules.
