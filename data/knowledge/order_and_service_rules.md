# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Orders, Service Fulfilment, and Trouble Management

## Order Lifecycle
- Submitted
- Accepted
- In progress
- Pending customer or external dependency
- Completed
- Failed
- Cancelled

## Service Trouble Lifecycle
- Reported
- Diagnosed
- Assigned
- In progress
- Resolved
- Closed
- Reopened

## Key Checks
- Order or trouble-ticket identifier
- Requested action
- Current status
- Status reason
- Appointment or promised date
- Dependency or blocking condition
- Impacted product and service
- Previous actions and timestamps

## Support Response
State the current status, what has already happened, what is blocking completion, and the next responsible action. Do not promise completion dates unless they are confirmed.
