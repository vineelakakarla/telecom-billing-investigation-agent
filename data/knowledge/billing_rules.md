# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Billing and Charging

## Purpose
Guide a customer-support agent in investigating billing questions across prepaid, postpaid, broadband, mobile, and converged services.

## Core Concepts
- Customer: the person or organization receiving service.
- Account: the financial or service relationship used for billing and account management.
- Product: the customer-facing commercial offering.
- Service: the technical or logical service delivered to the customer.
- Resource: the network or infrastructure resource used to deliver a service.
- Bill: a financial statement for a defined billing period.
- Bill item: an individual charge, credit, tax, adjustment, or payment allocation.
- Usage event: a record of service consumption that may be rated and charged.

## Common Billing Components
- Recurring subscription charges
- Usage and overage charges
- One-time activation or installation charges
- Equipment or device charges
- Roaming charges
- Taxes and regulatory fees
- Discounts, credits, and promotions
- Deposits and advance payments
- Adjustments and corrections
- Late-rated usage
- Proration after a plan change

## Standard Investigation
1. Confirm the customer and account.
2. Confirm the bill number and billing period.
3. Compare the current bill with the previous relevant bill.
4. Identify changed bill items.
5. Trace usage charges to related usage events.
6. Check product, plan, tariff, discount, tax, and effective dates.
7. Confirm whether the charge is recurring, one-time, prorated, adjusted, or late-rated.
8. Explain the evidence and identify the next action.

## Customer Support Controls
- Do not disclose another customer's information.
- Do not promise a refund or adjustment without authorization.
- Do not mark a charge as incorrect without evidence.
- Record the investigation result and supporting references.
- Escalate disputes that require financial approval, policy interpretation, or system correction.
