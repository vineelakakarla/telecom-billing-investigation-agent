# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Tax and Regulatory Charges

## Scope
Covers taxes, government levies, regulatory fees, and jurisdiction-specific charges.

## Key Checks
- Customer billing address and tax jurisdiction
- Charge type and taxable amount
- Tax rate or rule effective date
- Tax exemption status, if applicable
- Whether a discount changes the taxable base
- Whether the tax relates to a recurring, usage, or one-time charge

## Common Scenarios
- Tax increased because the underlying service charge increased.
- A new taxable item was added.
- A customer exemption expired or was not applied.
- A jurisdiction or billing address changed.
- Different products or charge types received different tax treatment.

## Support Response
Separate the underlying service charge from the tax amount. Do not state that a tax rate changed unless the approved policy or billing data confirms it.
