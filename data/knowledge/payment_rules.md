# Telecom Customer Support Knowledge: Payments and Collections

## Scope
Covers payment methods, payment allocation, failed payments, refunds, disputes, and collection status.

## Key Checks
- Payment identifier
- Payment status
- Payment date and amount
- Payment method
- Allocation to account or bill
- Reversal or refund status
- Failed-payment reason
- Collection or delinquency status

## Common Scenarios
- Payment received but not yet allocated.
- Payment allocated to a different bill or account.
- Payment failed or was reversed.
- Refund is pending approval or processing.
- A partial payment left an outstanding balance.
- A payment dispute is under investigation.

## Support Controls
- Never expose full card, bank, or authentication details.
- Do not promise a refund without the required approval.
- Explain payment status separately from bill status.
- Escalate suspected fraud, duplicate payment, and payment disputes.
