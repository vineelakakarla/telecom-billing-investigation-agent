# Trouble Tickets, Incidents, and Service Assurance

## Scope
Guidance for service interruptions, degraded performance, faults, and customer complaints.

## Required Checks
- Customer, account, and affected service
- Symptom and start time
- Service location or access identifier
- Known incident or outage
- Diagnostics already completed
- Impact and urgency
- Previous related tickets
- Restoration estimate, if officially available

## Customer-Support Policy
- Do not provide an unverified restoration time.
- Link duplicate reports to the existing incident or ticket where applicable.
- Record customer impact and troubleshooting results.
- Escalate safety-related, repeated, high-impact, or unresolved faults according to severity policy.
