# Payments, Refunds, and Collections

## Scope
Guidance for payment status, failed payments, refunds, balances, payment arrangements, and collection activity.

## Required Checks
- Payment reference and status
- Payment date and amount
- Payment method
- Invoice and account association
- Reversal, chargeback, or refund status
- Outstanding balance and due date
- Collection or payment-arrangement status

## Customer-Support Policy
- Never request or record full payment-card credentials.
- Do not promise a refund until it is approved and initiated.
- Explain pending, failed, reversed, and settled payments distinctly.
- Follow approved collection, payment-arrangement, and hardship procedures.
- Escalate suspected fraud, chargebacks, and unmatched payments.
